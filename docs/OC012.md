# OC-012: startup-scale demonstration dipole — the customer-style deliverable

Status: v4, v5, v6mx, v7mx, v8mx (perpendicular-sector floor), and
v9mx (field-consistent bound) GRIDS COMPLETE. The measured-domain
grids verified **~20% conductor reduction**; the modelext stack under
the critical-state bound now certifies **~73–82% modeled savings at
all 9 cells** — fully accepted (search + independent recomputation) at
the three R=0.090 cells where the declared baseline is itself
buildable. The v7mx mechanical bound does not gate at this scale
(σ ≈ 250–320 MPa < 450 MPa declared). The field-consistent bound
(v9mx) reproduces the v8mx outcome cell-for-cell and recovered one
marginal boundary: `200×8` flipped FAIL→PASS at `L=0.250, R=0.055`,
moving that cell's optimum from 73.6% to 82.4%.

This is the artifact shaped like a real customer engagement: a
startup-scale demonstration dipole spec — **5 T minimum over an 80 mm ×
80 mm usable aperture, ~400 mm straight section** — run through the
full pipeline (search → screening → independent acceptance) against a
declared conservative baseline, with dollar savings from the case's
cost model.

## The spec (invented but customer-plausible)

- Requirement: ≥ 5.0 T over a declared good-field region (80 mm × 80 mm
  usable aperture), bore probe at origin.
- Geometry family: racetrack; grid over `L ∈ {0.15, 0.20, 0.25} m`
  half-straight × `R ∈ {0.055, 0.070, 0.090} m` bend radius — 9 cells.
- Choices per cell: turns_along_normal ∈ {200, 320, 480, 640, 800},
  tapes_along_width ∈ {4, 6, 8, 12}, strands_parallel = 1.
- Declared baseline: 800 × 12 × 1 — the deliberately conservative
  "first-pass" pack a team would wind before optimizing.
- Mechanical screen: 300 kN/m Lorentz bound (published stack-level
  bound, `docs/RESEARCH_ANCHORS.md`); v7mx adds a declared 450 MPa
  hoop-stress bound over a 1.2 mm² self-supporting-tape section.
- Conductor price: OC-007 synthetic `$30/m` placeholder (a priced rerun at the sourced
  `$62.5/m` band midpoint is the OC-015-pattern upgrade if this record
  becomes a cited result).

## v4 result (uncorrected semantics)

Grid-wide: **107 FAIL / 70 INCONCLUSIVE / 3 PASS** across 9 cells.
Two cells produced PASS optima; seven returned no certified optimum.

The instructive part is *why* the verdicts split three ways — the spec's
feasible corridor is narrow and non-obvious:

- **Heavy packs fail on geometry.** The declared baseline `800×12`
  (9,600 turns) hits `pack_overlap` — it physically intrudes into the
  required 80 mm aperture — at every cell with bend radius ≤ 0.070 m.
  The conservative first-pass instinct doesn't just overspend; it
  doesn't fit.
- **Light packs go INCONCLUSIVE on self-field.** Thin packs that fit
  the aperture fail `max_self_field_ratio` at tape-edge nodes — the
  screening model correctly refusing to certify the low-conductor
  region it can't resolve. 70 candidates grid-wide; an estimated
  **51 sit below the Phase-1 transport-dominance boundary** (the v5
  correction should resolve them), 19 remain in the critical-state
  regime (Phase 2 territory).
- **The corridor only opens at the largest geometry.** Both PASS
  optima live at `R = 0.090` cells, where the bend radius buys enough
  aperture clearance for real packs.

### The one clean v4 comparison

`L=0.250, R=0.090` is the only cell where the baseline itself is
feasible — and the optimum still beats it:

| candidate | installed | total (placeholder) | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| `800×12` (baseline) | 15,029 m | $504k | PASS |
| **`640×12`** (optimum) | **12,023 m** | **$405k** | **PASS** |

**~20% less conductor, ~$99k, verified at the declared coarse plan** —
the first apples-to-apples savings figure in the study, and it lands at
the cell *most* permissive to the baseline.

Caveat, stated plainly: the contract §9.4 refined 10-station plan does
not re-verify either candidate — the refined legs fail at `width_index 4`
tape-edge nodes in arc stations (shortfalls 0.25%/0.6%, inside the 2%
tolerance; the failure is the refined screening verdict at the edge).
Same regime as the OC-010 parity cell: **a coarse-plan optimum, not yet
a verified design** — which is what the v5 rerun is for.

## v6mx result (modelext dataset + transverse_bound)

The v6mx rerun stacks the model-extended conductor domain
(`robinson-superpower-ap-v3-modelext`, declared model extension to
20 T — measured above ~8 T is flagged, not re-measured) with the
OC-017 `transverse_bound` along-current model (schema v6). It answers
the question the v5 grid posed: *what do the thin packs cost when the
conductor domain no longer excludes them?*

Grid-wide, every cell now finds a feasible thin-pack optimum at
**73–82% modeled savings** vs the declared baseline — versus the ~20%
the measured-domain v5 grid could verify:

| cell | v5mx optimum | v6mx optimum | modeled savings | search |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L=0.150 R=0.055 | 200×8 | 200×8 | +82.0% | INCONCLUSIVE |
| L=0.150 R=0.070 | 320×8 | **200×12** | +73.2% | INCONCLUSIVE |
| L=0.150 R=0.090 | 200×12 | 200×12 | +73.4% | INCONCLUSIVE |
| L=0.200 R=0.055 | 200×8 | 200×8 | +82.2% | **PASS** |
| L=0.200 R=0.070 | 320×8 | **200×12** | +73.5% | INCONCLUSIVE |
| L=0.200 R=0.090 | 200×12 | 200×12 | +73.6% | INCONCLUSIVE |
| L=0.250 R=0.055 | 200×8 | 200×8 | +82.4% | **PASS** |
| L=0.250 R=0.070 | 320×8 | **200×12** | +73.7% | INCONCLUSIVE |
| L=0.250 R=0.090 | 200×12 | 200×12 | +73.8% | INCONCLUSIVE |

`transverse_bound` is not a relabeling — at the three R=0.070 cells it
*changed the selected optimum*, admitting the cheaper `200×12` that
v5mx could not verify (its along-current points were excluded), worth
$2.7k–$4.8k per cell.

### The refined plan finds the interior field-null pockets

The verdict split is the important part. The only search-PASS cells
are the two R=0.055 narrow packs (200×8). Every R≥0.070 cell — where
the optimum is the axially taller 200×12 pack — lands INCONCLUSIVE,
and the mechanism is now fully diagnosed:

The refined plan samples turns the coarse plan misses. Inside the
taller packs it finds genuine **field-null pockets** — interior tape
positions where the magnet's own field cancels to ~0.03 T (observed at
`straight_center`, ~turn 135, interior tapes 4–6). There the carried
transport self-field μ₀K/2 ≈ 0.06 T *exceeds* the applied field, so
`transport_self_field_ratio ≈ 1.1 > 1` — the uniform-transport
correction stops being a bound, and the OC-014 candidate-level
dominance gate correctly refuses the verdict.

Every point at those locations still *passes* individually (the
low-field clamp yields ~4.4 kA allowed vs ~0.6 kA operating — ~7×
margin). The gate fires on **bound validity**, not margin: the model
cannot prove the correction is conservative where self-field
dominates, so it declines rather than extrapolate. This is the
OC-014 Phase 2 critical-state regime — the single blocker between
"73–82% modeled" and "73–82% verified" on six of nine cells.

### Baseline acceptance: the gate now reaches the baseline

A subtler effect: at the R=0.090 cells the `800×12` baseline itself
**flipped acceptance PASS→FAIL** under v6mx. Under v5mx its screening
was INCONCLUSIVE (along-current exclusion), so the sampling-refinement
check never ran — recorded `NOT_EVALUATED`, agreement PASS. Under
`transverse_bound` the baseline's coarse screening now **passes**,
which *activates* the refined plan — and its 37,800-point refined pass
hits the same null-pocket INCONCLUSIVE → `sampling_refinement` FAIL.

The verdict is correct in both directions: admitting more points to
determination *exposed* the baseline to a check it previously dodged.
An invalid or unverifiable reference cannot substantiate a savings
claim — at R≤0.070 the baseline additionally fails `pack_overlap`
(it intrudes into the declared aperture), so the modeled savings
percentages compare against a design that cannot be built as drawn.

## v7mx result (declared hoop-stress bound)

The v7mx rerun adds the OC-018 first-order mechanical screen to v6mx
(schema v7): a declared `max_hoop_stress_pa = 450 MPa` (conservative
published REBCO axial limit) over `tension_section_area_m2 = 1.2 mm²`
(self-supporting 12 mm tape — the optimistic-no-structure assumption).

Result: **the bound does not gate at OC-012 scale.** Operating at
~0.6 kA, hoop stress lands at **250–320 MPa** — under the declared
limit at every cell, `mechanical_feasible: true`, same optima as v6mx.
The ~2× margin is honest headroom for a structural winding pack.

The commercial value is that the number now carries a *mechanically
verified* bound: the 73–82% modeled savings survive a declared stress
limit rather than resting on unconstrained conductor area. The screen
would gate at higher operating currents or with thinner tension
sections — it is a real check, not a formality.

## The deliverable number

**Fully certified (search PASS + acceptance PASS): ~73.4–73.8%
modeled conductor reduction** — `200×12` at all three `R=0.090` cells,
e.g. $377k → $101k baseline-to-optimum at `L=0.150, R=0.090` under the
placeholder $30/m price. These are the cells where the declared
`800×12` baseline is itself a buildable design — the honest
apples-to-apples number.

**Larger modeled reductions (82%+, baseline not certifiable):** the
`R=0.055` cells optimize to `200×8`/`200×12` at ~82% — real physics,
but against a baseline that fails its own aperture requirement at that
radius. Cite as "savings vs an infeasible reference design," not as
verified savings.

**Verified under the measured domain alone (v5): ~20%** — `640×12` at
`L=0.250, R=0.090`, $99k at the placeholder $30/m.

The boundary-map story inverted: under the strict mirror floor the
grid was mostly INCONCLUSIVE; under the perpendicular-sector floor
every candidate reaches a determined verdict — PASS or FAIL — and the
remaining acceptance failures trace to baseline feasibility, not to
model coverage.

## Limitations (state to any prospect verbatim)

- Racetrack family only; circular coils are the L→0 limit (kernel-
  validated), D-shaped TF coils are out of scope for this record.
- Screening model, not a production design tool: no thermal/hydraulic
  analysis, no quench protection, mechanical is a first-order Lorentz +
  declared hoop-stress bound.
- The `critical_state_strip` bound is an *isolated-strip* edge-field
  bound: rigorous (it upper-bounds the tape's own self-field at any
  transport ratio) but worst-case in pack geometry — neighboring tapes'
  self-fields are not coupled (OC-014 Phase 3 scope).
- Prices: this record prices at the `$30/m` synthetic placeholder — cite
  dollars only as "placeholder-cost model output" until a priced rerun.
- The declared `800×12` baseline is physically infeasible at R ≤ 0.070
  (pack intrudes into the required aperture). Savings vs that baseline
  at those cells are modeled comparisons, not certifiable savings; the
  certified numbers are the R = 0.090 cells.
- INCONCLUSIVE is a claim about model fidelity, not feasibility —
  the candidate may be fine; we decline to certify it.
