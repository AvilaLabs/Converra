# CR-01 — Estimating tape performance from sparse measurements

Roadmap item: [CR-01](../ROADMAP.md#cr-01--estimate-tape-performance-from-sparse-measurements).
Status: **protocol frozen; no scored run yet.**

## Question

Can a small subset of a REBCO tape's critical-current data, of the kind a reel-to-reel in-field scanner or a
short in-field sample provides, predict that tape's withheld critical current at 20–40 K and 5–8 T in
perpendicular field?

The answer determines what evidence a reel passport must contain before Converra can rely on it for allocation.

## Data

- **Source:** the public Robinson Research Institute critical-current database, figshare collection 2861821, CC BY 4.0.
- **Coverage:** 20 REBCO tape products, plus one YBCO film on sapphire that is reported separately, not in the
  headline.
- **Preparation:** downloaded workbooks were converted by `tools/cr01_prepare_robinson.py` into Converra's
  measurement columns. The originals and prepared files are kept locally under ignored
  `.local/research/cr01/robinson/`. Their identities are recorded in `sources.json` there.
- **Conversion check:** for the four products already embedded in `data/materials`, every overlapping row
  matched the embedded data exactly.

## Frozen protocol

[`benchmarks/cr01/protocol-v1.json`](../benchmarks/cr01/protocol-v1.json), SHA-256
`f09d8331cdab54b6e4421d5fd11d186dc14e2adc1e3f817f9f3b1eb65e33915c`. It was frozen before any critical-current
value was fitted or summarised.

| Scenario | Inputs used for prediction | Expectation declared before the run |
| --- | --- | --- |
| S0 — 77 K self-field (control) | Self-field Ic at 77.5 K and a leave-one-product-out population lift factor | FAIL |
| S1 — scanner | Perpendicular-field data at 65–77.5 K, 0.5–4 T | Uncertain |
| S2 — scanner plus anchor | S1 plus one perpendicular point at 30 K, 3 T | Better than S1 |

**Model.** S1 and S2 use the three-parameter scaling `ln Ic = c0 − T/T* − α ln B` (Senatore et al. 2016),
fitted by least squares with a 95% prediction interval. A four-parameter variant is reported for diagnosis
only and cannot change a verdict.

**Product verdict.** A product PASSES when it meets both limits:

- worst overprediction ≤ 10%;
- 95th-percentile absolute error ≤ 20%.

Overprediction is limited more tightly because overpredicted capacity is unsafe in allocation.

**Scenario verdict.** A scenario PASSES when at least 80% of evaluated tape products pass. Per-product
verdicts are the primary result. Products missing required inputs are NOT_EVALUATED, never dropped.

**Evidence class.** Registered retrospective comparison. Earlier Converra studies used the 20–40 K data of the
four already-embedded products, so those four are also reported as a separate stratum.
