## What the number actually says — read honestly

- The optimum and baseline carry *identical* conductor metres: both wind
  600 total turns (NI is fixed by the field requirement; the search
  solved the same operating current for both). The conductor cost is
  $92,123.89 + $9,212.39 scrap in **both** — the entire $700 delta is
  assembly/joint cost from the cheaper pack shape (2 pancakes vs 3).
- So OC-013 is a *savings-floor* result at sourced pricing: the
  optimizer matched the baseline's conductor exactly and took the only
  delta available inside this case's geometry space. It demonstrates
  the dollar pipeline end-to-end at a defensible price — it does NOT
  demonstrate conductor savings (this case's physics answer is "the
  baseline already used the right tape-metres").
- At $62.5/m the conductor is ~90% of total cost — the economics are
  conductor-dominated, as expected; the placeholder assembly/joint
  prices (~$1.5k) are noise next to it. Where conductor savings exist
  (OC-010's ~3% vs an expert design, or spec-sensitivity deltas), the
  sourced price converts them honestly.
- The v4→v5 path is open: rerunning this case under
  `limits.self_field_correction` would produce the corrected-semantics
  priced comparison whenever a v5 priced case is wanted (the candidate
  verdicts here are not self-field-limited, so the numbers are expected
  to be unchanged — worth verifying, not assuming).
