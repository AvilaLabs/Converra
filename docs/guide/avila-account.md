# Avila Labs account (optional)

Converra can sign you in to an Avila Labs account. Signing in is optional, and every Converra feature works without it.

## What signing in does today

One sign-in covers ACTINV, Converra and OpenBNCT. In the browser workbench, use **Sign in** or answer the first-visit prompt. On the desktop app, sign-in uses a device code that you approve in your browser. A tool launcher links to the other Avila Labs tools.

Nothing from your work is sent: no inputs, results or files leave your device because you signed in. Calculations still run locally.

## Where the sign-in is kept

The desktop app stores its token in `~/.config/avila/credentials.json`. Signing out revokes the token and removes the file's credentials.

## Without an account

Skip the prompt or never press **Sign in**. Cases, imports, searches, studies, exports and review packages behave the same.
