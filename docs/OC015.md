## The honest reading — and why it matters

OC-013's clean acceptance PASS was partially **unearned**: under v4 the
self-field gate inspected only the utilization-limiting point, so the
refined plan's edge-node verdicts were never scrutinized. Under v5 the
dominance boundary is evaluated at *every* point — and the edge regime
the coarse plan skips is exactly where scrutiny lands.

So the correction cost us a headline: "0.68% verified, acceptance PASS"
(v4) became "0.68% modeled, refined-plan verification unresolved at the
tape edge" (v5). That is the machinery working as designed — a claim
that got *more* honest, not a result that got worse. The design intent
stands: no verdict is accepted on evidence the plan didn't produce.

## Consequence for claims

Any cited OC-013/OC-007 acceptance figure should carry the caveat that
it predates the corrected edge scrutiny; the verified-savings claim
under current semantics is OC-012's coarse-plan ~20% (which carries the
same refined-plan caveat) and OC-013's rerun is pending the same edge
regime — either the along-current-fraction limit or the
transport-dominance bound at the refined-sampled edge nodes.
