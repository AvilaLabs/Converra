# OC-024 — NHMFL 32 T magnet field parity

`benchmarks/coupled/oc-024a.json`, `oc-024b.json` · `tools/oc024_32t_parity.py` · records written to `runs/oc024/` (ignored)

## Purpose

OC-010's ~3% conductor parity against Feather-M2 is at low field. OC-024
extends the credential to the regime customers actually operate in: the
NHMFL **32 T all-superconducting magnet**, the physically built and
commissioned user magnet whose REBCO insert contributes 17 T at the bore
center (a separately powered 15 T LTS outsert supplies the rest).

The claim under test is narrow and checkable: *given the built winding
geometry and operating current, does OptCoil's field kernel reproduce the
insert's measured center-field contribution?* Material screening is not
under test — every conductor point sits above the embedded datasets' 8 T
ceiling and correctly reports INCONCLUSIVE. That wall is itself the
finding: a 32 T-class magnet's conductor utilization cannot be verified
on public measured data, only its field model can.

## Reference data (published)

From Weijers et al. (NHMFL 32 T magnet technology slides) and IEEE TASC
22(3) 4300704 / successors:

- Two nested dry-wound REBCO double-pancake coils, series-powered.
  Inner: IR/OR 20/70 mm, height 178 mm, 20 DP modules (40 pancakes),
  2.8 km tape. Outer: IR/OR 82/116 mm, height 320 mm, 36 DP modules
  (72 pancakes), 6.6 km tape.
- 4 mm SuperPower tape with co-wound insulated steel; commissioned
  operating current 173 A (design spec 180 A); Jave 176 / 196 A/mm².
- HTS contribution at bore center: **17 T** at operating current.

The sources do not tabulate turns directly; two derivations are carried
and reported as a band — tape-length wound (70 m / 91.7 m per pancake at
mean circumference) and published Jave over the winding section.

## Model mapping

Each coil is a 360° circular-path pack at the winding mid-radius
(45 mm / 99 mm), `tape_normal: radial`: `turns_along_normal` is the
radial spiral turns per pancake at the tape-thickness pitch
(0.202 mm inner / 0.231 mm outer — each coil's radial extent over its
own turn count), and `tapes_along_width` is the axial pancake count at
the pancake pitch (4.45 mm, tape + co-wind). One case per coil; kernel
linearity makes the bore fields additive. The outer coil runs its real
147 turns × 72 pancakes; the width-axis sampling cap
(`MAX_SAMPLED_TAPE_INDICES = 256`) was raised for exactly this class —
the turn-axis cap stays at the contract's shared 64.

Screening is INCONCLUSIVE by construction — conductor fields sit above
the 8 T data ceiling — and `search_status: FAIL` on both runs is the
honest record of that: this is a field-parity exercise, not a certified
design. Operating temperature is declared at 25 K (strictly interior to
the dataset span; the built magnet runs 4.2 K) — the field parity is
temperature-independent.

## Results

Unit bore field per ampere-turn (recorded `unit_bore_bz_t_per_ampere_turn`,
kernel-evaluated at the origin probe):

| Coil | Modeled pack | unit field |
|---|---|---|
| Inner | 248 turns × 40 positions (R 20–70 mm, H 178 mm) | 6.2725e-6 T/(A·t) |
| Outer | 147 turns × 72 positions (R 82–116 mm, H 320 mm) | 3.3385e-6 T/(A·t) |

Predicted HTS contribution = Σ unit field × turns × I_op:

| NI basis | I_op | Predicted | vs 17 T |
|---|---|---|---|
| tape length | 173 A | 16.88 T | **−0.7%** |
| published Jave | 173 A | 16.94 T | **−0.3%** |
| tape length | 180 A | 17.56 T | +3.3% |
| published Jave | 180 A | 16.94 T | **−0.3%** |

Both run records carry `acceptance.agreement_status: PASS` on the
baseline — the independent recomputation agrees with the search-side
field evaluation.

## Findings

1. **The kernel tracks a real 32 T magnet to within ~1%.** At the
   commissioned 173 A the predicted insert contribution is 16.9 T against
   the published 17 T — within 0.7% on the tape-length basis and 0.3% on
   the Jave basis. Combined with OC-011's Bluemira cross-check and
   OC-010's low-field conductor parity, the field model now has a
   built-magnet credential in the >20 T regime.
2. **The data wall is the real result.** Every sampled conductor point is
   INCONCLUSIVE — the insert's conductors see ~17–30 T, far above the 8 T
   measured-data ceiling. Field parity is achievable from public
   geometry; *conductor* parity (the OC-010 metric) is not, until
   measured Ic(B,T,θ) exists above ~20 T. That is the high-field measured-data gap this record makes explicit.
3. **A latent work limit surfaced and was fixed.** The generated
   sampling plan's width axis shared the turn axis's 64-index cap —
   coil 2's 72 pancakes could not be enumerated. `coupled::Sampling`
   now carries a separate `MAX_SAMPLED_TAPE_INDICES = 256`; the turn
   axis keeps the frozen 64 the refined-plan contract fixes, so no
   search-semantics change results. An NI-preserving regroup
   (63 × 168 turns) run beforehand confirmed the distribution effect
   at the bore is below printed precision — the unit field is
   bit-identical to the real 147 × 72 geometry.
4. **Series power simplifies nothing else.** Both coils share 173 A; the
   two packs were modeled and summed — the schema has no multi-pack
   construct, and linearity makes the decomposition exact.

## Known limitations

- Turn counts are *derived* from published tape length or Jave — the
  primary sources do not tabulate them; both derivations are reported.
- Axial current distribution is uniform per pancake position; real
  pancakes have per-module spacing variations and the inner coil's
  self-field/screening currents at 17 T+ perturb the true distribution.
- No LTS outsert model — the 15 T contribution is not computed; the
  parity covers the REBCO insert's published share only.
- `tape_width_m` carries the pancake pitch (4.45 mm), not the 4 mm tape —
  screening quantities scale on it, but screening is INCONCLUSIVE on
  coverage regardless; the field computation uses conductor positions
  only.
- Screening, joints, protection, mechanics: all unverified by design —
  the record's INCONCLUSIVEs are the accurate statement of coverage.
