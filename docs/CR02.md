# CR-02 — Reel passport schema and per-reel inventory

Roadmap item: [CR-02](../ROADMAP.md#cr-02--reel-passport-schema-and-per-reel-inventory).
Status: **schema, validation and rating implemented in the engine, CLI, Python and MCP (2026-10-04). The synthetic
inventory generator remains.**

## Purpose

A reel passport records what is known about one physical reel of tape and where that knowledge came from.
An inventory is a set of passports. Converra rates each reel at an operating point from its passport and
the product's critical-current map. Each rating states its evidence class and, when it cannot be given,
the reason and the evidence that would allow it.

## What CR-01 requires of the design

[CR-01](CR01.md) found that:

- 77 K self-field values and 65–77 K scanner data, converted by a scaling law or a population factor, do not
  rate tape at 20–40 K;
- low-temperature data predicts well inside its measured field range and overpredicts beyond it.

The design therefore follows three rules:

1. **Every rating comes from a product map that covers the operating point.** The map is a material dataset
   bound by id and CSV SHA-256. Converra's existing interpolator refuses points outside measured cells, and
   that refusal is kept. There is no field or temperature extrapolation.
2. **A reel's length profile only scales the product map.** The scale factor at position x is the profile's
   Ic at x divided by the map's Ic at the profile's measurement condition. Applying that factor at the
   operating point assumes that a reel's deviation at the profile condition carries over to the operating
   point. CR-01 did not test this, because the Robinson data has one sample per product. Every scaled rating
   is therefore labelled `model_informed`, and its `basis` field states the assumption.
3. **In-field points measured on the reel are evidence, not adjustments.** A short-sample point at or near the
   operating point is compared with the scaled rating and reported as a consistency check. It does not
   silently replace the rating.

## Passport — `optcoil-reel-passport/v1`

JSON with `deny_unknown_fields`. SI units, and currents are whole-tape currents in amperes as measured.

| Field | Content |
| --- | --- |
| `schema` | `"optcoil-reel-passport/v1"` |
| `reel_id` | Non-empty identifier, unique within an inventory |
| `evidence_class` | `measured`, `model_informed` or `synthetic`. It must be no stronger than the weakest section class: any `synthetic` section makes the passport `synthetic`. |
| `product` | `vendor`, `product`, `batch` (nullable), `product_map` (nullable `{dataset_id, csv_sha256}`) |
| `geometry` | `length_m` > 0, `width_m` > 0 |
| `length_profiles[]` | Fields: `id`, `method` (free text, e.g. `"reel-to-reel transport"`), `evidence_class`, `temperature_k`, `field_t`, `angle_from_normal_deg`, `electric_field_criterion_v_per_m`, `resolution_m`, `source_sha256` (nullable), and `points`: `[[position_m, ic_a], …]`. Positions strictly increase within `[0, length_m]`; `ic_a` ≥ 0 and finite. |
| `length_profiles[].map_reference` | Optional, nullable `{source_row}`: the issuer declares which row of the product map represents the profile's condition. This is needed because the interpolator evaluates 0 T only at exact measured coordinates, as for 77 K self-field reel scans. The row must exist in the map. Its nominal coordinates must match the profile condition within these tolerances, or the reference is rejected: temperature within 1.0 K; field both 0 T, or within 2% relative; angle within 5°, unless both fields are 0 T; criterion equal. |
| `in_field_points[]` | `sample_id`, `position_m`, `temperature_k`, `field_t`, `angle_from_normal_deg`, `ic_a`, `n_value` (nullable), `electric_field_criterion_v_per_m`, `lab`, `evidence_class`, `source_sha256` (nullable) |
| `ab_offsets[]` | `position_m`, `offset_deg`, `uncertainty_deg` ≥ 0, `method` (`xrd_rocking_curve`, `transport`, `torque`, `other`), `evidence_class` |
| `defects[]` | `start_m` ≤ `end_m` within the reel; `kind` (`dropout`, `delamination`, `mechanical`, `splice`, `other`); `action` (`excluded`, `cut`, `accepted`); `min_ic_fraction` (nullable, 0–1); `note` |
| `provenance` | `issuer`, `issued_at` (RFC 3339 date or date-time), `sources[]` of `{description, sha256}` |
| `limitations[]` | Free-text statements carried into every output |

**Identity.** `passport_sha256` is the SHA-256 of the passport file's exact bytes, as for dataset bundles.
Signing passports is left to CR-07, which defines the open reel-evidence format.

## Inventory — `optcoil-reel-inventory/v1`

| Field | Content |
| --- | --- |
| `schema` | `"optcoil-reel-inventory/v1"` |
| `inventory_id` | Non-empty |
| `evidence_class` | No stronger than its weakest passport |
| `passports[]` | Embedded passport objects; `reel_id` unique |
| `limitations[]` | Free text |

Validation also reports totals: reel count, total and usable length, length by product, and counts by evidence
class. Usable length is length minus the defect spans whose action is `excluded` or `cut`; overlapping spans
count once.

## Rating — `optcoil-reel-rating/v1`

**Input.** An inventory, an operating point (`temperature_k`, `field_t`, `angle_from_normal_deg`,
`electric_field_criterion_v_per_m`), and the material datasets for every `product_map`. The datasets come
from the embedded store or from supplied bundles, with the identity contract enforced.

**Output per reel.** A status, an evidence class, a `basis` string, an `explanation`, and the rated values
below. Every status other than `rated` must carry a non-empty explanation and a `next_evidence` statement
saying what evidence would allow a rating.

The rules apply in order; the first that fires decides the status.

1. **`no_product_map`.** The passport has no `product_map`.
2. **`map_unavailable`.** The map's dataset is not supplied or does not match its id and SHA-256.
3. **`outside_map_domain`.** The map cannot evaluate the operating point: the interpolator returns no
   estimate, or the criterion differs. This is where an unmeasured field, for example 12 T against an 8 T
   map, stops.
4. **`orientation_unresolved`.** The operating angle is within 15° of the tape plane (|θ − 90°| < 15°) and
   the passport has no `ab_offsets`. A few degrees of ab-plane offset can change Ic there by up to about 20%
   (Cheng et al. 2025).
5. **Orientation with offsets.** In that window with offsets present, the map is evaluated at θ − |o| and
   θ + |o|, and the smaller value is used. Here o is the largest |offset| + uncertainty on the reel. The
   reel's orientation in the coil is not chosen until CR-03; this choice is the conservative one until then.
   If either angle is outside the map's domain, the status is `outside_map_domain`.
6. **Product-level rating.** With no length profile, the reel's rating is the map value times the reel width,
   with no per-reel information. Status: `rated_product_level`. The evidence class is the map's class,
   capped at `model_informed` when the map is not `measured`.
7. **Scaled rating.** With a length profile, take the profile whose condition the map can evaluate and that
   comes first in the passport. If no profile's condition can be evaluated, the status is
   `profile_unscalable`.
   - A profile with a valid `map_reference` is evaluable. Its map value at the profile condition is the
     referenced row's `ic_a_per_m`, and the `basis` names the row. A profile whose reference fails the
     tolerance check is not evaluable, and the explanation says why.
   - Scale factor at each profile point: s(x) = `ic_a(x)` / (map value at the profile condition × width).
   - Rated Ic(x) = s(x) × map value at the operating point × width.
   - Report: the minimum, 5th percentile and median of rated Ic over profile points in usable length; the
     position of the minimum; and the minimum within each defect span whose action is `accepted`.
   - Status: `rated`. Evidence class: `model_informed`, or `synthetic` if the passport or the map is
     synthetic.
   - The record's limitations quote the published within-product transfer scatter: about 15% (1σ) between
     77 K self-field and 20 K, 20 T (Molodyk et al. 2021).

**Consistency check.** Each in-field point whose temperature is within 0.5 K, field within 2% and angle within
2.5° of the operating point is reported with:

- the measured `ic_a`;
- the scaled rating interpolated linearly at that point's position;
- the relative difference between the two.

It is a check and changes no status.

**What the rating is not.** It is not an engineering current allowance and not an acceptance. Rating records
use the existing verdict vocabulary only where a gate exists. The per-reel statuses above are descriptive.

## Interfaces

- **Engine.** Schema types and validation live in `optcoil-model::reel`. The rating lives in
  `optcoil-search::reel` and uses the existing material interpolator.
- **CLI.**
  - `optcoil reel validate PASSPORT`
  - `optcoil inventory validate INVENTORY`
  - `optcoil inventory rate INVENTORY --temperature-k --field-t --angle-deg [--criterion-v-per-m] [--dataset-bundle …] [--output]`
- **Python and MCP.** The same three operations, added after the engine and CLI.

## Known gap: no full-range product maps are embedded

The embedded measured maps cover 20–40 K only. No embedded dataset has rows at 77 K or at 0 T. A real 77 K
self-field reel scan therefore cannot be scaled against an embedded map, and such a reel is reported as
`profile_unscalable`. Supplying a full-range product map as a dataset bundle closes the gap; the public
Robinson datasets cover 77 K self-field. Embedding full-range maps is a separate, versioned dataset change.

## Published variation statistics

Retrieved copies are kept in ignored `.local/research/tape-allocation/`.

| Quantity | Published value | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Along-length variation of 77 K Ic within one tape | Relative standard deviation fell from about 10% to 2–3% for some manufacturers | Jaroszynski et al. 2025, §4.3 |
| Low-temperature lengthwise variation | Short samples cut every 20 m from an edge-slit tape differ by up to 50% at B ∥ ab and about 10% at 30° (4.2 K, 30 T). Samples from a centre-slit tape overlap. | Jaroszynski et al. 2025, Fig. 9 |
| Within-product ratio of 20 K, 20 T Ic to 77 K self-field Ic | About 15% standard deviation over 200 production samples of one product, described as more reproducible than more complex formulations | Molodyk et al. 2021, Fig. 4a |
| Scaling from 77 K to 4.2 K | Uncertainty over 60%, for other formulations | Jaroszynski et al. 2025, citing Braccini et al. |
| Lot spread of 77 K self-field Ic | Mean 175 A per 4 mm over about 300 km; best 10% above 200 A | Molodyk et al. 2021, Methods |
| ab-plane offset per reel | Group means −1.81°, +3.25°, −1.09° (SD 0.53°, 0.11°, 0.58°); 2.2° ± 0.5° over 68 samples in an earlier study; bimodal over 359 samples from one vendor | Cheng et al. 2025, Table 2 and §I |

**Consequence for scaled ratings (rule 7).** CR-01 could not test within-product transfer. Molodyk et al.
report it for one favourable product: about 15% (1σ) between 77 K self-field and 20 K, 20 T. A rating scaled
from a 77 K reel profile therefore carries at least that uncertainty at fusion operating points. That exceeds
CR-01's 10% overprediction limit. Scaled ratings stay `model_informed`, and the 15% figure is quoted in their
limitations.

## Synthetic inventories

Synthetic inventories are generated from the statistics above and labelled `synthetic` in every output. Each
statistic used is recorded with its source in the inventory's `limitations`. A quantity with no published
value must be supplied by the user and is labelled as such, never defaulted. The correlation length of
along-length variation is one example.

For each reel:

1. Draw a lot factor for the 77 K self-field level. The default standard deviation of 11% is derived from
   Molodyk's mean and 90th percentile under an assumed normal distribution, and is labelled as derived.
2. Draw an along-length 77 K profile with the declared relative standard deviation (2–3%, user-chosen within
   the published range) and the user-supplied correlation length.
3. Draw a hidden low-temperature transfer factor, lognormal with 15% standard deviation. Synthetic inventories
   carry this "truth" separately from the passport, so that CR-03 can measure how allocations behave when the
   scaled rating is wrong.
4. Draw an ab-plane offset from a chosen published group.
