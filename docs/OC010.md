# OC-010 — Feather-M2 real-coil benchmark

`crates/optcoil-search/examples/oc010_featherm2.rs` · runs written to `runs/oc010-featherm2*`

## Purpose

OC-007 through OC-009 measure OptCoil against a deliberately oversized
synthetic baseline; OC-010 measures it against a physically built and
tested magnet: **Feather-M2** (CERN/EuCARD2), a HTS racetrack dipole.

The question: *given the same requirement, does OptCoil's verified
optimum use less conductor than what a real magnet program actually
wound?* The comparison metric is 12 mm-equivalent tape-metres, not
dollars — the cost ledger's prices remain placeholder — because
conductor consumption is price-independent.

## Reference data and provenance

Feather-M2 (from CERN-ACC-NOTE-2017-0045 and the IEEE/TASC powering
papers; the quantities below are documented, but the 190 m tape figure
is *derived*, not quoted verbatim from a primary table):

- Aligned-block racetrack dipole, ~800 mm long, ~250 mm straight-section
  scale, **40 mm clear bore**.
- Two poles × two decks, wound from a **Roebel cable of ~15 strands**
  of ~2 mm REBCO (Sunam/SuperOx low-performance batch), sharing
  ~6.5 kA in parallel.
- **Achieved 3.1 T at 6.5 kA / 5.7 K** standalone in helium gas; the 5 T
  design target was not reached with the tested tape batch.
- Derived tape usage ≈ **190 m of 12 mm-equivalent** (15 × 2 mm strands ≈
  2.5 × 12 mm-equivalent width, × cable length over turns and decks).
  Stated as a band, not a point — the value is approximate.

Benchmark choices:

- The benchmark targets the **achieved 3.1 T** specification, not the
  5 T target — the material dataset's validated coverage tops out near
  8 T and a 5 T aperture would push conductor fields toward or beyond
  it.
- The good-field region is 40 mm (y) × 40 mm (z) at the bore center,
  with x-extent held inside the straight section (0.8·L) so the usable
  volume never overlaps the bend annulus.
- `b_target_t` = 3.1 T, temperature 5.7 K, the OC-007 dataset and
  sampling plan otherwise.

## Cable-mode result (schema v4, `strands_parallel`)

At (0.125, 0.024) — the cell where no single-strand pack could carry
the current — parallelism opened the feasible set:

| Pack | Strands | Installed tape | Status |
|------|---------|----------------|--------|
| 40×4 | 4 | 416.5 m | PASS (best) |
| 40×4 | 8 | 833.0 m | PASS |
| 40×4 | 15 | 1,561.9 m | PASS |
| 60×4 | 4 | 624.8 m | PASS |
| 60×4 | 8 | 1,249.5 m | PASS |
| 60×4 | 15 | 2,342.9 m | PASS |

**Strands buy feasibility, not savings.** Capacity scales with
conductor-metres exactly (s × tape buys s × current), so the optimizer
never prefers a cable where series suffices — the cheapest strand-pass
(416 m) is 2.3× the 184 m single-strand optimum at (0.10, 0.028).
Feather-M2's real 15-strand Roebel was exactly this trade; the model
now reproduces the mechanism and prices it honestly.

## Findings

1. **Parity, not a blowout.** The single-tape model's best screened
   design uses ~3% less tape than the real coil. The model reproduces a
   real conductor mass within measurement precision — strong evidence
   the physics and constraint machinery track reality; weak evidence of
   hidden savings *at this spec*.
2. **The bore is the binding constraint.** R = 0.024 returned no
   feasible pack: n ≥ 80 packs overlap the 40 mm bore (pack-overlap
   gate), and thin packs that clear it could not carry 3.1 T at
   single-tape currents.
3. **Feather-M2's own escape was the cable.** Their 15-strand Roebel
   cable shares 6.5 kA in parallel — a degree of freedom the v3 model
   did not have. This motivated schema v4 `strands_parallel`.
4. **The small-R corner hits a fidelity boundary, not a capacity
   boundary.** The `wide` probe (thin-n × wide-p) found candidates that
   pass requirement and geometry but go INCONCLUSIVE on the tape-edge
   self-field ratio — the evaluator cannot verify the screening-current
   regime. Parallel strands do not lift that gate. **Update:** OC-014
   Phase 1 (schema v5, `limits.self_field_correction =
   "uniform_transport"`) now resolves the ratio ≲1 band via the
   uniform-transport-sheet bound; OC-010's ratio-5 cluster is
   self-field-dominated (transport ratio >1) and correctly stays
   INCONCLUSIVE pending the Phase 2 critical-state model — see
   `docs/OC014.md`.

## Modes

- `./oc010_featherm2` — the 3×3 (L, R) grid, single-tape packs.
- `./oc010_featherm2 wide` — thin-n × wide-p packs at the small-R corner.
- `./oc010_featherm2 cable` — schema v4 strands (1/4/8/15) at the
  small-R corner: tests whether current-sharing ever pays its extra
  tape-metres.

## Known limitations

- Single-tape-per-turn footprint: `strands_parallel` models current
  sharing with *full-width* tapes; a real Roebel's 2 mm strands have
  ~1/6 the per-strand Ic. Strand costs in tape-metres are comparable,
  capacities are not exactly — comparisons at s > 1 are indicated,
  not exact.
- The self-field-ratio gate at tape edges bounds the small-R region
  (see finding 4).
- No end-winding, transposition loss, insulation, or joint-resistance
  terms beyond the declared cost ledger.
