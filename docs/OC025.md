# OC-025 — SPARC TFMC public-record reconstruction + vendor bake-off

`benchmarks/coupled/oc-025-tfmc.json` · `benchmarks/coupled/oc-025-tfmc-bakeoff.json` · records written to `runs/` (ignored)

## Purpose

OC-024 anchored field parity against a built magnet (NHMFL 32 T) but left
screening INCONCLUSIVE by construction — its conductor fields exceed every
embedded dataset's 8 T ceiling. OC-025 attempts the stronger claim on the
most documented HTS magnet in the literature: the **SPARC Toroidal Field
Model Coil (TFMC)**, whose full parameter table is public.

The question under test: *reconstructing the TFMC winding from published
parameters only, does the as-built configuration screen, and does the
procurement ledger reproduce the published conductor quantity?* A second
question runs as a vendor bake-off on the same case: *does the verdict
hold on every measured commercial dataset, or only the declared one?*

## Reference data (published)

From Hartwig et al., IEEE TASC 33(5) 3332613 (2023); Vieira et al., IEEE
TASC 34(5) 3356571 (2024); IAEA FEC 2023 synopsis:

- Winding: **16 pancakes × 16 turns = 256 turns** (stack-in-plate)
- **270 km REBCO** installed, **40.5 kA** terminal current, **20.1 T** peak
  field on conductor, 20 K supercritical helium operation
- Minimum winding-pack turn radius **0.2 m**; magnet envelope ~1.9 × 2.9 m;
  winding-pack mass ≈ 5113 kg; ~250 tapes per turn-stack (derived from the
  published tape total and mean turn length)

## Model mapping

- Closed D-like path: straight inner leg (0.558 m) + two 90° corner arcs
  (R = 0.44 m centerline) + one 180° outer arc (R = 0.719 m). The pack is
  centered on the path, so the inner pack edge sits at ≈ 0.2 m — the
  published minimum turn radius. Path length ≈ 4.20 m/turn, the published
  *mean* turn length.
- `turns_along_normal = 16` (radial turns per pancake),
  `tapes_along_width = 16` (pancakes in the axial stack),
  `strands_parallel = 250` (parallel tapes sharing the turn current —
  the VIPER-class stack abstraction).
- Requirement: modeled bore-field target **5.0 T** at the D-bore-center
  probe (−0.580, 0, 0). This is a declared proxy requirement — the real
  program's binding constraint (20.1 T peak on conductor / stress / NI)
  is not modeled. We do **not** claim to reproduce 20.1 T or 40.5 kA.
- Operating temperature **25 K** — strictly interior to the dataset span
  (the real coil runs 20 K).
- Dataset `robinson-superpower-ap-v3-modelext`: Wimbush/Strickland/Pantoja
  measured SuperPower AP characterization (figshare 4256624, CC-BY) with a
  declared model extension; fields beyond the measured ~8 T rely on the
  extension, labeled `measured_with_model_extension` in the record.
- `limits.along_current_model = "transverse_bound"`: declared conservative
  bound for points where the field aligns with transport current beyond
  the measured angular model — bounded estimates, never silent
  extrapolation.

## Results

As-built search (`oc-025-tfmc.json`, one candidate 16×16×250):

- Requirement PASS, refinement PASS, screening PASS (2240 points, 0
  unsupported/excluded), `search_status PASS`, independent acceptance
  **agreement PASS** with 0.0 sampling-refinement shortfall.
- NI solve: I_op = 23.33 kA at the declared 5 T target; modeled peak
  sampled field 5.787 T.
- Installed conductor **268.8 km** vs published 270 km; purchased
  295.7 km at the declared 10% scrap; modeled cost $8.88 M at the
  case's synthetic $30/m.

An earlier envelope-scale variant (matched the *magnet* envelope rather
than the winding-pack mean turn) solved I_op = 36.8 kA against the
documented 40.5 kA — ~9% — without any length fitting.

Vendor bake-off (`oc-025-tfmc-bakeoff.json`, same case, only the dataset
mutated, 7 embedded datasets):

|---|---|---|---|---|
| robinson-superpower-ap-v3-modelext | measured+ext | **PASS/PASS** | 1.84% | PASS |
| robinson-superpower-ap-v3 | measured | **PASS/PASS** | 1.84% | PASS |
| robinson-shanghai-hflt-v3 | measured | INCONCLUSIVE/FAIL | 1.63% | 1022 unsupported |

Dense-plan diagnostics (`OPTCOIL_TRACE_UNSUPPORTED`, the refined
13-station/9-turn plan folded into the declared sampling) attribute every
INCONCLUSIVE to a distinct dataset property, not to tape performance:

- **Shanghai HFLT**: the dataset's own monotonicity audit FAILs (4
  violating pairs, worst +2.3% Ic increase with field vs the declared
  0.1% tolerance). With the audit failed, the declared
  `monotone_field_lower_bound` policy cannot certify any sub-1 T point —
  all 1022 demote to unsupported. The tape's measured margins at the
  operating point are fine (util 1.63%); the dataset cannot underwrite
  the low-field interior.
- **THEVA AP**: audit PASS, and its 1006 low-field lower-bound points
  stand — but 18 points at field angle θ ≈ 0°/180° require mirror-pair
  angle representations (e.g. 179.89°, −0.11°) beyond the measured
  angular range (−0.10°…179.67°) → unsupported.
- **FFJ YBCO**: same angular-edge mechanism, 8 points (its measured
  angular floor 0.06° does not cover the 0.02°-class representations).
  Its 98-pair audit failure is *latent* here — its 0.01 T measured floor
  makes the sub-1 T queries estimates, so no lower-bound points existed
  to demote. Its coarse utilization 0.68% is the strongest of the four —
  at equal declared price it would be the margin winner if its dataset
  covered the edge angles.
- **Babouche MEM fits**: declared field support 8–16 T sits above this
  case's ~5.8 T modeled peak; the rows correctly report they cannot
  answer rather than extrapolating down.
- **SuperPower modelext**: passes on extension-covered estimates; its own
  audit technically FAILs (3 pairs, worst 0.18%, in the extension region)
  but no lower-bound point existed for the demotion to touch — latent,
  recorded here for honesty.

## Findings

1. **The as-built TFMC configuration screens clean end-to-end** — the
   only dataset among seven to sustain the independent refined plan is
   SuperPower AP's, whose characterization happens to be the case's own.
   The design point is real; the coverage is dataset-limited.
2. **The 268.8 km vs 270 km agreement is an internal-consistency check,
   not a prediction.** The modeled mean turn length was derived from the
   published 270 km; the ledger correctly reproduces its own input. The
   defensible independent comparison is the envelope-scale run's ~9%
   operating-current agreement.
3. **The bake-off's discriminator was dataset quality, not tape
   quality.** A naive Ic(B,T,θ) lookup would have ranked FFJ first on
   margin; the honest machinery reports FFJ's dataset cannot certify the
   design at refined coverage. Three distinct coverage mechanisms fired
   (non-monotone measured data, angular-grid edge extent, field-range
   floor) — exactly the silent-extrapolation traps the verdict
   semantics exist to catch.
4. **This is a public-data reconstruction.** It is not customer
   validation, not manufacturing qualification, and not a reproduction
   of the TFMC's actual binding constraints (peak conductor field,
   mechanical stress), which this model does not carry.

## Known limitations

- The requirement is a declared bore-field proxy; the real 20.1 T
  peak-on-conductor / 40.5 kA operating point is not modeled.
- The D path approximates the stack-in-plate winding; tape width and
  radial pitch are inferred, not published.
- The 270 km figure is reproduced by construction (see Findings).
- 25 K evaluation stands in for the real 20 K operating point for
  dataset-interiority reasons; the Babouche fits do cover 20 K but not
  this case's field range.
- All prices are declared synthetics; the bake-off compares coverage and
  margin, not supplier economics.
- INCONCLUSIVE rows are dataset-coverage verdicts. Whether a re-measured
  or denser angular/low-field characterization of those tapes would pass
  is unknown — the datasets as published cannot answer.
