# OC-031 — Non-planar (path3d) helix screening under a declared field map

`benchmarks/coupled/oc-031-helix-layer.json` · `tools/oc031_helix_fieldmap.py` · `benchmarks/fieldmaps/oc031-helix-map-source.csv` · records written to `runs/oc031/` (ignored)

## Purpose

Search schema v19 ships `fixed_geometry.path3d` — a `CoilPath3D`
centerline of helix segments (the CORC/CCT-class winding primitive) —
and v13/v14 ship the customer-declared Cartesian `field_map` that a
non-planar pack requires (the engine's self-field evaluator is planar
and is gated off by schema). Before this record, no shipped benchmark
exercised either: the slice was implemented and unit-tested but never
run end-to-end on a realistic winding.

## Geometry and field source

- **Winding**: a dense-wound helical solenoid layer — 50 turns at
  4 mm/turn rise (one tape width) on a 25 mm former, pack extents
  4 mm (cylinder-radial) × 8 mm (in-surface binormal); baseline 8×1.
- **Field map**: 14,580-node Cartesian grid of the winding's *own*
  field at reference NI = 10⁴ A·turns, computed by
  `tools/oc031_helix_fieldmap.py` — an independent Biot-Savart
  evaluation smearing the reference ampere-turns over 8 offset
  filaments across the pack band. Not the engine's kernel.
- **Semantic constraint discovered**: the engine scales declared-map
  entries linearly with candidate ampere-turns, so a static environment
  field (e.g. a background solenoid) *cannot* be honestly baked into
  map entries — it would mis-scale with NI. The shipped map contains
  winding self-field only. Cases needing a background field must wait
  for a schema extension that separates the two components.
- Material: `robinson-superpower-ap-v3` (measured, 20–40 K),
  `T_op` = 21 K, utilization limit 0.8.

## Result (4 candidates, threads=2, ~0.1 s)

| candidate | NI (A·turns) | I_op | peak field | util | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4×1 | 10,000 | 2500 A | 3.15 T | 1.99 | FAIL |
| 4×2 | 10,000 | 1250 A | 3.15 T | 1.99 | FAIL |
| 8×1 | 10,000 | 1250 A | 3.15 T | 0.78 | INCONCLUSIVE |
| 8×2 | 10,000 | 625 A | 3.15 T | 0.78 | INCONCLUSIVE |

Full pipeline verification: requirement Pass (NI solves exactly to the
map's reference), refinement Pass, acceptance agreement Pass — cost and
field recomputed independently at 0.000 error.

## The empty-window finding

No candidate configuration PASSes, and tuning can't produce one:

- **Thin packs** (4 layers): utilization ~2× the limit — physically
  correct overcurrent at 3 T / 21 K.
- **Thick packs** (8 layers): utilization 0.78 — under the limit — but
  5–7 sampled points at the helix ends sit below the dataset's 1 T
  field floor → `lower_bound` → INCONCLUSIVE.
- Clearing the floor needs peak field ≳4.6 T (helix end field is
  ~35% of mid-pack); at that level utilization is ~1.2 → FAIL.

A helical winding's end-region field dip is physical — the floor-vs-
utilization window is empty *on this geometry*. That's the honest
answer a screening tool should give: not "yes" or "no," but "the
measured data cannot certify the end turns."

Also demonstrated: `along_current_excluded` points (6–14 per candidate)
where the helix's field runs parallel to the tape — a geometric
property planar windings don't have.

## Known limitations

- Field map is nearest-node lookup on a 4–5 mm grid — pack-adjacent
  field gradients (~mm scale near filaments) are smeared by the
  8-filament model, not resolved.
- No helical-pack self-field solve, screening-current model, or
  3D good-field-volume — declared-map screening of a non-planar
  geometry, not engineering acceptance (per ARCHITECTURE.md's v19
  scope note).
- The dataset floor binding the helix ends (≤1 T at low T) is the
  same low-B × low-T gap OC-030 identified — unclosable with published
  data today.
