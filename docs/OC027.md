# OC-027 — piece-length procurement model (schema v24)

`benchmarks/coupled/oc-027-tfmc-pieces.json` is the OC-025 TFMC
reconstruction upgraded to schema v24 to exercise the piece-quantized
procurement ledger: `cost.piece_policy` + a base `piece_offerings`
catalogue, `boundary: per_module` (pancake-stack reality — pieces reset
at module boundaries), `piece_unit: per_strand` (each of the 250
VIPER-stack strands buys and splices its own tape), `splice_cost_usd`
$500, catalogue {70 m @ $32, 300 m @ $30, 600 m @ $28}, all
`price_source: synthetic`.

## What it demonstrates

The as-built winding needs ~74.3 m of required strand per pancake
(67.5 m installed + 10% declared attrition). The ledger argmin compares
the offerings on *spend* — pieces × length × price + splice cost:

- 70 m @ $32: 2 pieces per strand-pancake → 8,000 pieces, 4,000
  in-winding splices, 560 km purchased, **$19.93M total**.
- 300 m @ $30: 1 piece per strand-pancake → zero splices but 1.2M m
  purchased — ~$36M in remnant-driven waste. Cheaper per metre, far
  more expensive per magnet.
- 600 m @ $28: worse still.

The chosen plan buys the shorter, *premium-priced* piece because remnant
dominates price-per-metre — the procurement tradeoff the legacy flat
`scrap_fraction` ledger could not express. Joint cost splits as
15 module interfaces ($3,000) + 4,000 piece splices ($2,000,000).

## Checks

- `cargo test -p optcoil-search piece_` — 8 tests: per-module and
  continuous hand-calculated ledgers, per-spec catalogue argmin,
  `per_strand` vs `conductor_unit` count scaling, schema gating,
  and end-to-end agreement with the independent acceptance
  recomputation (`search_acceptance.rs`, checker v17).
- `optcoil coupled-search oc-027-tfmc-pieces.json` — PASS requirement,
  screening, refinement, acceptance; `optcoil bom` reports the piece
  plan with `totals_agree: true`.

## Limits

Offerings and splice cost are synthetic placeholders — the model's
arithmetic is exercised, not a vendor's price list. Splice resistance,
QA yield, lead lengths, insulation and freight remain unmodeled; the
piece walk currently covers radial-normal, no-field-map cases only.
