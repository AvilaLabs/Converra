# OC-016 — labeled model extension above 8 T; the coverage-blocked prize measured

**Status: sensitivity study complete. Every number below is model-informed,
not measured-data-verified.**

## The question

OC-012's v5 grid left 54 of 70 INCONCLUSIVE candidates blocked solely by
dataset coverage (peak conductor fields 9–17 T vs the measured 8.0 T
ceiling). The question: would the thin packs *pass* if the material data
reached that far — and how much conductor is on the table?

## The instrument

`robinson-superpower-ap-v3-modelext` (schema v2,
`data_class: measured_with_model_extension`): all 3,225 measured rows
byte-identical + 860 modeled rows at 10/12.5/16/20 T. Anchored power law
per (T, angle) curve, `alpha = max(3–8 T tail fit, local 7→8 T slope,
0.1)`, uniform ~26% conservative margin from held-out edge checks
(the family over-predicts the measured 7/8 T edge by ≤20.9% across all
215 curves; margin = bound + 5% pad). Produced by
`tools/prepare_oc016.py`; full provenance in the dataset's
`preparation-audit.json` and README.

**Headline:** at the only cell where the declared baseline is feasible,
the model-informed optimum cuts installed conductor from 15,029 m to
3,757 m — **a 75.0% reduction** ($372k at the $30/m placeholder, ~$775k
in the $62.5/m sourced band). Winning utilization 0.67 — 33% headroom
remains *after* the conservative margin.

## What changed in the blocker map

- Coverage-blocked candidates: **54 → 0**. The extension resolved every
  one to a determined verdict.
- Along-current-bound: 17 remain INCONCLUSIVE — unchanged, as expected;
  that is a field-*direction* limit, not magnitude. The oblique-field
  Ic model is now the only standing blocker class.
- 3 of 9 cells reached full `PASS` search status (every candidate
  resolved); the rest still carry along-current stragglers.

## What this is not

- **Not verified savings.** The modeled nodes are an unvalidated
  continuation. The margin makes them conservative *if the power-law
  family is right*; nothing bounds a different high-field mechanism.
  This is the size of the prize, not the prize.
- Acceptance `agreement_status` still FAILs on the §9.4 refined-plan
  edge legs — unchanged from the measured-data runs.
- Per-width rescale from the 1 mm bridge to a commercial tape is
  unchanged and still an assumption.

## What it means

The design space under the coverage wall contains ~75% reductions, not
~20%. The binding constraint on claiming them is a *measurement* — the
UNIGE/Tohoku SuperPower tables (19/24 T) are the exact shape needed
(request in flight). If real data lands within the margin, these
verdicts convert to measured-data PASSes by re-pinning the dataset id —
no code change, just a new provenance.
