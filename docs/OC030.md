# OC-030 — Babouche MEM-fit v2 high-field reach check

`benchmarks/coupled/oc-030-memfit-v2-reach.json` · `tools/prepare_babouche_memfit.py` · records written to `runs/oc030/` (ignored)

## Purpose

`babouche-*-memfit-v2` datasets extend the emitted grid toward the
published MEM parameter quad's 19 T corner (Babouche et al. 2026,
doi:10.1088/1361-6668/ae940e): B up to 19 T at 20 K, 17–18 T across
15–25 K, versus v1's ≤16 T. This record asks the narrow question: *does
the extension actually resolve points v1 leaves unsupported?*

## Method

No shipped case queries the 16–19 T band — OC-024's NHMFL-insert parity
geometry peaks at 8.53 T conductor field, and OC-025's TFMC peaks near
6 T. `oc-030-memfit-v2-reach.json` is a declared synthetic derivative of
`oc-024a.json`: identical circular coil geometry (R = 45 mm, 248 turns ×
40 tapes, radial tape normal), operating temperature lowered 25 → 20 K
(the quad's widest row), and the bore requirement raised 8.5 → 17.0 T so
the refinement drives peak conductor field into the v2-only band.

It is not a machine claim: nothing about it corresponds to a built
device, and its verdicts are model-informed (`published_model_fit`), not
measured-data-verified.

## Result (single candidate 248×40, T = 20 K)

| dataset | peak sampled field | determined points | unsupported | screening |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `babouche-superpower-m31477-memfit-v1` | 17.07 T | **192** | 608 | INCONCLUSIVE |
| `babouche-superpower-m31477-memfit-v2` | 17.07 T | **400** | 400 | INCONCLUSIVE |

The v2 extension resolves **+208 points** (108% more) — the points whose
fields land in 16–19 T at ~20 K, exactly the wedge the larger grid adds.
Utilization on shared coverage is identical (0.289), confirming the
extension changes coverage, not answers.

Both candidates still compose to FAIL — driven by refinement/mechanics
at 17 T bore, orthogonal to the dataset — and screening stays
INCONCLUSIVE under both datasets because 400+ points remain unsupported.

## The floor is the real wall

The unsupported residue is not at the ceiling — it is *below the babouche
datasets' 8 T field floor*. The UNIGE campaign's published quad starts at
7.5 T; it measured no low-field regime. The same floor failure appears in
the OC-025 vendor bake-off ("field floor 8 T > case"). Consequences:

- The babouche datasets are **complements, not standalone coil-screening
  datasets**: a coil's sampled points span 0–peak T, and most sit below
  8 T. `robinson-superpower-ap-v3-modelext` remains the only embedded
  dataset covering a high-field case end to end (OC-024a: 800/800
  determined, PASS) because it stacks a measured 0.05–8 T floor under a
  declared model continuation.
- The value of v2 is a second, independent model-informed opinion in the
  16–19 T wedge — genuinely different provenance (a published angular
  anisotropy fit) from the anchored power law of modelext.
- Closing the real gap wants **measured low-field data at low T**
  (below ~8 T at 5–35 K), which neither Robinson (20–40 K, but measured)
  nor Babouche (low T but not low B) currently supplies.

`oc-025-tfmc-bakeoff.json` now also enumerates the two `-v2` ids, so the
vendor bake-off exercises them wherever it runs.
